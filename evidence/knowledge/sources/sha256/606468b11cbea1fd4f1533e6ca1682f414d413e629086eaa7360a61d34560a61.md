# Agreement on netting

- Cashflows received by Ratan can be netted together by FMO in Ratan cashflow blotter.
- Netting can be done by FMO in cashflow blotter GUI or daily scheduled auto netting job, netting is not allowed after cashflow sent to Razor.
- Netting eligibility rules will be setup in Ratan, cashflows hit these rules would be NSTP and tagged with specific indicator then FMO can easily recognize these cashflows. 1. SCI netting flag as part of client onboarding 2. iCDMS netting agreement document
- FMO can perform Adhoc Netting on these cashflow in 'Projected'/'Queued'/'Pending'/'Validated' Status.
- Net Preview required to show the projected cashflow, FMO can view the projected result and decide to discard the netting action or submit.
- Netting on netting resultant cashflow is not considered, FMO would manually un-net and rennet on the latest component cashflows.
- The netting resultant cashflow would be NSTP and request the FMO manual review, the underlying netting component cashflow is visible from the GUI.
- FMO can manually perform the un-net on the resultant cashflow in 'Projected'/'Queued'/'Pending'/'Validated' Status which are not sent to Razor yet.
- If netting resultant cashflow not handled on value date, system will move the cashflow to 'Failed' status.
