# Resolve knowledge questions

Input: material ambiguity, missing rule, conflict, stale claim, or owner decision with its evidence and consequence.
Output: sourced answers with confidence and meaningful remaining questions; authorized review work uses one living question document.
Clarification supplies no publication approval or passing business-execution evidence.
For read-only requests, return questions and proposed updates without writing documents, findings, answers, or records.
Use durable question and record updates below only within existing authorization.

## Investigate and ask

1. Investigate existing knowledge, original intent sources, exact implementation, reports, and retained observations before asking.
2. Answer resolved questions with confidence, source links, rationale, and material limits using [the investigation rules](../../../tasks/README.md#investigate-before-asking).
3. Keep unanswered policy separate from missing execution or deployment evidence. Do not infer business intent from working code.
4. For authorized review work, reuse one living question document. Keep business questions and investigation evidence under the owning task.
5. Put short topic narratives beside their questions. Explain the consequence, responsible owner, and decision needed.
6. Provide clearly proposed options and an editable answer field. Permit another answer, unknown, or a named contact.

Do not preselect a business answer or replace a recorded answer. Ask only questions available investigation cannot resolve confidently.
Hand over the editable document for asynchronous completion. Resume affected ingestion when returned answers and continuation are requested.
Preserve unanswered questions. A continuation request supplies no missing business answer or publication approval.

## Apply returned answers

Preserve exact answers and their source before further interpretation or investigation.
Record decision authority, applicable scope, environment or version, changed meaning, and remaining limits.
When an answer resolves target meaning, use [record composition](../compose-knowledge-records/SKILL.md) to update the affected candidate or successor.
Preserve predecessor identity, source provenance, and review history. Add sourced measurable Gherkin criteria when required behavior changes.
Link the question, retained answer, affected record, finding resolution, and task knowledge-update log before handoff.
The log identifies versions, criterion IDs, findings, and audit references. Record `not applicable` with a reason for unchanged domain knowledge.

Keep actual behavior and delivery status in independent task gaps and evidence. Knowledge may link those gaps for navigation.
Keep changed content as a candidate until required review and publication gates pass.
Return to [claim review](../review-knowledge-claims/SKILL.md), then [transition preparation](../prepare-knowledge-transition/SKILL.md) for authorized governance changes.
Stop affected meaning changes while a material answer remains unresolved. Continue independent evidence collection within scope.
