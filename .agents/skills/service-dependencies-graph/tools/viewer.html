What does “unresolved” mean?
Resolved
The extractor matched consumer and provider evidence within this graph.
Declared only
The source declares a local relationship. A provider match is not established.
Unresolved
A declaration exists, but the extractor cannot identify its provider. Configuration, dynamic values, or missing source may prevent a match.
Ambiguous
More than one provider matches. The graph does not select one.
Candidate
The relationship is inferred and requires source review.

“Confirmed” describes a source declaration. It does not mean that its provider was resolved.

Unresolved does not mean broken. An external or unknown target is not proof of a third-party service.

Priority controls presentation depth. Business criticality requires separate evidence and review.
