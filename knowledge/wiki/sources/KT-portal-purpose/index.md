## Summary

The candidate describes a common entry and workspace for post-trade applications.
Shared platform functions support tenant teams, which retain their domain responsibilities.
See the canonical [summary](../../../candidates/background/KT-portal-purpose.md#summary).

## Key rules and qualifiers

- Affirmation, confirmation, settlements, and reporting are stated examples. They do not establish a complete application inventory.
  See [business purpose](../../../candidates/background/KT-portal-purpose.md#business-purpose).
- A tile is an application view. A workspace holds opened tiles.
  See [working vocabulary](../../../candidates/background/KT-portal-purpose.md#working-vocabulary).
- The capability map includes identity, composition, access, administration, integration, observation, and interoperability.
  It does not establish deployed or tested coverage.
  See [capability boundaries](../../../candidates/background/KT-portal-purpose.md#capability-boundaries).
- The entry-to-workspace flow is a synthesis of documented capabilities, without retained execution evidence.
  See [user and application flow](../../../candidates/background/KT-portal-purpose.md#user-and-application-flow).
- Draft principles and 2026–2027 proposals remain distinct from accepted scope and current implementation.
  See [scope and roadmap](../../../candidates/background/KT-portal-purpose.md#scope-and-roadmap).
