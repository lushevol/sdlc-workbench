# RATAN trade migration payment controls

## Summary

## Documented knowledge

### Overlap and target principle

The retained 2026 approach describes seven business days of overlapping Murex and Stella cashflows. It identifies duplicate payment, accounting, and LMS risk. Its principle cancels Murex cashflows and settles Stella cashflows. Block in-scope Murex Monday/Tuesday payments in advance through NSTP. Trade cancellation should then cancel them. Block corresponding Stella payments over the migration weekend, and remove that block only after reconciliation passes. This preserves subsequent Stella market-event handling. Accurate Murex cancellation IDs arrive Saturday; Stella IDs arrive Sunday. Portfolio-based blocking can therefore include out-of-scope cashflows.

### NDS fixing identity gap and environment boundary

The PROD approach records a duplicate-payment risk from Murex-generated FXD fixing trades for NDIRS and NDCCS. These have different trade and original-trade IDs from the parent and usually arise at VD-2. Precise advance blocking conditions are absent. Stella instead generates deliverable-currency cashflows directly. The Murex FXD trades are not migrated and should cancel after migration. The source identifies this as newly understood after the November 2025 migration. Its PROD procedure is not the UAT/DR procedure: secured dumps can already contain released or settled cashflows.

### Stella rule and reconciliation release

The PROD portfolio approach creates the Stella NSTP rule on Saturday. Conditions include source Stella or Ratan, next Monday/Tuesday payment dates, and in-scope portfolio. After Sunday reconciliation passes, disable the rule and suppress then unsuppress affected cashflows. This reprocesses them through ordinary BAU behavior. It establishes a planned sequence, without proof that reconciliation passed.

### HKTW historical variant

The November 2025 HKTW runbook compares the earlier China approach with a new high-risk NSTP approach. Both block Murex payments until cancellation and route settlement to Stella. Already-arrived Murex cashflows require HOLD. The HKTW plan marks earlier cashflow-suppression alternatives struck through. ### April 2026 DR sequence

The DR runbook secures a dated data dump and in-scope portfolio list, then analyzes future-seven-day Murex breakpoints. Its UAT/DR-only rule suppresses Murex withdrawal events for the stated payment dates; CANCELLED has priority over CASHFLOW_SUPPRESSED. It can suppress previously RELEASED or SETTLED cashflows in this controlled environment. Stella high-risk NSTP uses source, Monday/Tuesday dates, and portfolio. Inputs include final cancellation IDs, Murex payment lists and totals, BAU lists for broken reconciliation, FXD-to-parent NDS mapping, Stella payment lists, and Murex-to-Stella trade mapping. Export and reconcile future-seven-day feeds, run auto-netting, verify grouped/blotter totals and no pending grouping cashflows, and reconcile cancellation. Disable Stella NSTP, then Ops suppresses and unsuppresses the identified high-risk Stella cashflows. The runbook status cells do not prove completed actions.

### May 2026 PROD sequence

The go-live runbook establishes Murex high-risk NSTP before materialization using source, Monday/Tuesday payment dates, and portfolio. Monitor Pending Auto Netting, Pending Netting, and Pending Another Leg because they can bypass that rule. Ops must not net in advance. Incorrectly released or settled Murex cashflows become WAITING with Reversal after cancellation; the runbook then calls for releasing the withdrawal and Stella cashflows. Saturday inputs include final cancellation and payment lists, FXD-parent mapping, migration details, and Stella trade mapping. Narrow the Murex rule from portfolio to cancelled trade or original-trade IDs. Suppress and unsuppress out-of-scope cashflows so normal BAU can resume. Establish Stella high-risk NSTP. Sunday steps stop auto-netting, monitor Stella feeds, export future-seven-day cashflows, restart auto-netting, and export again. Reconcile Stella/RATAN feed totals and Stella/Murex future-seven-day cashflows. Disable both NSTP rules, monitor Murex cancellations, and reconcile cancellation. Ops reviews reconciliation and reprocesses high-risk Stella cashflows by suppression and unsuppression. Recorded sample counts are documentary observations, without retained execution evidence.
