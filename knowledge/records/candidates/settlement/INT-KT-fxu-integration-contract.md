# FX utilization phases and integration contract

## Scope

Proposed FXU lifecycle, Blade controls, data access, accounting, settlement-method actions, and version-specific message design.
These source descriptions define separate contracts. They establish no deployed or tested behavior.

## Summary

The selected sources describe related responsibilities and conditions.
Their original authority, effective dates, and current applicability remain unresolved.

## Documented knowledge

### FXU-01 — Market expansion dependencies

The expansion list requests trade-level GROSS↔UTIL changes and hard blocks after any full or partial utilization.
It requests early utilization, time-option, cancellation-charge, and unutilized-trade cancellation workflows.
It requires UTIL stamping for future cashflows and remaining-amount visibility in Blade.
Utilization within an allowed forward window updates utilization status immediately; accounting waits until value date.
The list includes FMO and Middle Office hard blocks. It supplies no approval or market-by-market adoption decision.

[Selected original evidence](../../../evidence/sources/sha256/53f751c573eb0c247e99679ce1198d20a9b2e24f9a3ee3d532f6879f94193e8e.md#L1-L13).

### FXU-02 — Blade data-access decision remains open

The analysis identifies RATAN as the utilization golden source for Blade controls and remaining amounts.
It compares three options: replicate through Stella/TDS3, query RATAN directly, or display RATAN through OpenFin.
Replication preserves Blade's single data source but adds latency, model scope, reconciliation, and downstream impact.
Direct calls reduce replication latency but couple Blade to RATAN availability, performance, and data merging.
OpenFin supports human visibility; it does not supply Blade backend controls.
No option is selected by the retained analysis. Claims of guaranteed data correctness within an option are rationale, not execution proof.

[Selected original evidence](../../../evidence/sources/sha256/ec9cd28af3302a3c8369ab3e0e171aaa5de4c9499447d178770ca64e0dcb9860.md#L6-L51).

### FXU-03 — MVP utilization scope

The MVP proposal covers FX Spot, Forward, and Swap, with client eligibility and auto-utilization static data.
Upstream/static rules or manual booking select UTIL. A utilized trade needs a hard block, with reversal before amendment.
This block applies to all profiles, including Middle Office, for utilized or partially utilized cashflows.
UTIL must not settle gross. MVP accepts full-utilization requests and excludes partial, reversal, early, and past-due features.
FXU queries RATAN, sends utilization through Solace, and receives ACK/NACK through Solace.
RATAN maintains remaining amounts for FXU and Blade and records whether the trigger was FXU, automatic, or manual.
The proposal sends utilization information to TLM, RATAN EOD, SSDR, and FMMIS.
Manual utilization directly through an ad hoc SSI action is struck out.
The proposal requires explicit agreement on mandatory fields and rejection predicates.
Its proposed EOD full-utilization time `17:00 GMT` is TBC and must not override later entity timing.

[Selected original evidence](../../../evidence/sources/sha256/7998224ec4847783c625c3358614b030450c01cd52c6c01284652e7bb8a7e920.md#L18-L32).

### FXU-04 — Phase 2 accounting and time scope

Phase 2 proposes contract-level partial, past-due, reversal, and early utilization.
Partial utilization posts only the utilized amount to FXBRREC; residual amounts post to PastDue at cutoff.
Further activity reverses applicable PastDue accounting before new utilization accounting. Reversal restores the remaining amount.
PastDue substatus persists after cutoff until full utilization. Cancellation reverses PastDue accounting. Aging measures elapsed time from value date.
Early requests can start at VD-10 in the stated phase. Remaining amount changes on receipt; eBBS accounting waits until value date.
Earlier windows for other countries and automatic swaps remain to be discussed.
PastDue and auto-utilization cutoffs are entity-configurable, with PastDue after auto-utilization.
The listed GMT/local pairs are EG `16:30/18:30`, SA `16:30/19:30`, and NP `15:15/21:00`.
These are source-specific proposals, not a universal current calendar. The numeric accounting walkthroughs are examples, not fixed business amounts.

[Selected original evidence](../../../evidence/sources/sha256/7998224ec4847783c625c3358614b030450c01cd52c6c01284652e7bb8a7e920.md#L38-L50).

### FXU-05 — Settlement-method action predicates

UTIL→GROSS is proposed for WAITING, READY, or PASTDUE. It sets remaining amount to zero, removes PastDue substatus, and reinstates gross settlement.
PastDue accounting reverses after the change. The functional page calls this a cashflow-level action.
GROSS or blank→UTIL requires WAITING or `READY+NA+NA`, an external data source, eligible FX taxonomy, and non-reversal reason.
Eligible taxonomies are `ForeignExchange:Forward`, `ForeignExchange:Spot`, and `ForeignExchange:Swap`.
The action sets remaining amount from payment amount and stamps settlement means from client static.
Selecting one cashflow expands to its trade's cashflows, with a warning when displayed count differs from initial selection.
The page specifies a bulk limit of `100 trade/cashflow`, trade-ID sorting, and per-trade success/failure responses.
Its action granularity and bulk denominator require reconciliation with later technical and scenario drafts.

[Selected original evidence](../../../evidence/sources/sha256/ae5f959718f0d5f247e2dfae7ab08060cf921fd9ac885c41b8c5688442ce5c6f.md#L1-L31).

### FXU-06 — Validation and rejection contract

The response table requires Utilization_Id and ACK/NACK, and lists source-specific Error_Reason values.
Validation covers UTIL scope, trade major version, cancellation, cashflow errors, expected product leg counts, request types, amount equality, duplicates, currencies, and account means.
Expected non-cancelled/error cashflow counts are two for Spot/Forward and four for Swap.
Required request content includes the Utilization and Trade objects, ID, type, comments, payment reference, maker, checker, trade ID, major version, currency, and amount.
Swap-leg validation must use the product-specific contract; the row wording does not define every valid blank value.
Settlement means and account must be FXBRREC or FXBRREC-M.
The table marks reversal, past-due, early, and partial rejection rows as MVP limits or phase-specific rules.
It marks several scenarios not possible; those labels do not prove runtime unreachability.
Business NACKs generally route to the FXU FMO exception queue. FMO/PSS investigation and manual Oscar handling depend on the failure.
Phase 2 reversal/past-due predicates must not be combined with MVP rejection rules into one timeless validator.

### FXU-07 — Draft failure and method-change design

The phase-2 draft responds to malformed JSON with NACK and `Raw message error.`, retaining Raw_Request for diagnosis.
It proposes DLQ retry at most five times before a `Ratan internal error.` NACK.
The method-change proposal takes effect immediately at trade level and supports bulk operations.
UTIL→GROSS immediately reverses existing PastDue accounting. Withdrawal method inherits the latest New event's value.
The draft maps a released UTIL→GROSS withdrawal to READY plus Utilization.
The fuller design separately maps the released gross withdrawal to WAITING(Reversal). These draft outcomes remain unresolved.
The proposed POST endpoint is `/v1/utilization/cashflow/settlementMethod/stamping`.
Its request groups cashflow IDs by trade and includes settlementMethod and comment. Results report per-trade success and errorMessage.

[Selected original evidence](../../../evidence/sources/sha256/880f9412fccdcab988fa7b5733fefdfaf8c0fccf8a6736fe73999a1c3d977942.md#L55-L191).

### FXU-09 — Utilization processing and state design

The detailed proposal uses separate utilization, reversal, PastDue, and PastDue-reversal actions with validator chains.
The common flow validates, updates remaining amount, moves status, saves history, and publishes accounting events.
It proposes cashflow-level distributed locking and database transactions, with separate cross-system consistency controls.
The selected design choice reuses domain-event status synchronization for Utilize, PartiallyUtilize, and Unutilize to Stella.
The glossary says FULLY_UTILIZED, while the transition table uses UTILIZED. These names are not silently treated as aliases.
READY can become UTILIZED, PARTIALLY_UTILIZED, or PASTDUE according to action. FullReverse returns utilized or partially utilized flows to READY.
Withdrawal of utilized or partially utilized cashflows produces ERROR; withdrawal of PASTDUE produces CANCELLED.
Some partial actions from PARTIALLY_UTILIZED without PastDue are struck out. Later PastDue partial rows are marked added on 4.1.
The table therefore requires version-specific interpretation, not one complete current state machine.
UTIL workflow intent bypasses Vostro validation and stamping and avoids SWIFT generation. Nostro data comes from utilization static.

[Selected original evidence](../../../evidence/sources/sha256/c43f5f2cbbf3385239a7f5d2ede689bb1b67709ed1bbf8d4c2672decbfebca95.md#L3-L180).

### FXU-10 — PastDue and accounting identity design

PastDueReverse accompanies utilization/reversal when PastDue accounting exists. It also applies after cancellation or UTIL→GROSS.
The proposed PastDue static uses settlement means FXBRREC and settlement account PASTDUE; the actual account value remains a placeholder.
For failed FXU acknowledgements or accounting publication when Kafka is unavailable, the proposal uses PostgreSQL-backed retry.
Utilization Service supplies accounting external-system-key so Accounting Service can identify utilization reversals. Its length must be less than 50.
Manual keys follow `fxu.{utilizationId}.{cashflowId}`; automatic and PastDue keys follow `fxu.{snowflakeId}.{cashflowId}`.
These publication retries differ from the draft's five internal-error DLQ retries and do not establish one combined retry policy.
The selected design leaves the FXU ACK boundary open, including later eBBS rejection.

[Selected original evidence](../../../evidence/sources/sha256/c43f5f2cbbf3385239a7f5d2ede689bb1b67709ed1bbf8d4c2672decbfebca95.md#L194-L277).

### FXU-11 — Method-change inheritance and draft APIs

The detailed proposal processes method changes immediately at trade level and inherits the latest New method into Withdrawal.
It proposes propagating ERROR into a new event when the same trade already has ERROR with utilized history.
This remains a proposed protection against amendment release; its acceptance and transaction boundary are unresolved.
The cashflow query reuses the GUI API with tradeId and s2bxId parameters.
Currency2 lookup uses `/api/ratan/v1/fx/utilization/trade/getCurrency2ByCurrency1` with trade major version, leg, currency1, and amount1.
The design applies RATAN rounding configuration; it supplies no rounding precision to invent.
The response envelope contains status, message, and data. Examples distinguish success, validation failure, and internal error with null data on failure.
The internal remaining-amount POST accepts cashflow IDs and returns an ID-to-amount map.
Static APIs provide utilization configuration and eligible-rule query, creation, deletion, approval, rejection, and audit.
Configuration binds booking entity and counterparty with automatic/manual intent and FXBRREC/FXBRREC-M settlement data.
Sample customers, IDs, amounts, timestamps, and malformed example JSON are not production data or a complete schema.

### FXU-RECOVERY — Technical-readiness recovery requirements

The 2026 readiness scope names EG, NP, and SA FX Spot, Forward, and Swap.
It requires persistent delivery and ServiceNow alerts within five minutes. Uber has no ACK/NACK; FXU does.
The design requires automatic connection recovery, graceful shutdown, and Message Bridge republishing.
Malformed Uber format retries are bounded before dropping. Network retries continue until success.
Invalid upstream Uber messages carry upstream errors and require user handling through ET.
SDK mismatch calls for ECR handling and TDSX replay. These paths do not permit silently accepting invalid data.
FXU exceptions return descriptive NACKs. Upstream retries failed queries.
Technical utilization failure restores READY with a NACK so upstream can retry.
Accounting publication recovery remains distinct from utilization acceptance and downstream business acknowledgement.
Source-reported deployment nodes, database tables, and job implementation are excluded from this intended contract.

### FXU-PHASE2 — Companion pre-CAB scope and ambiguity

The companion Phase 2 checklist requires contract-level partial, past-due, reversal, and early utilization.
Utilized amounts debit FXBRREC and credit Bridge. EOD residual amounts debit PastDue and credit Bridge.
Later utilization reverses applicable residual PastDue posting before the new utilization posting.
Reversal debits Bridge and credits FXBRREC, restoring the remaining amount.
The source requires testing automatic-utilization reversal and exposes remaining amounts through the DQSL/SSDR API.
Early utilization accounting waits until start of value date.
Its PastDue heading describes cancellation of previous utilization, while the body describes residual accounting. This ambiguity remains unresolved.
Entity timing is configurable and unconfirmed in this source. It does not override another source with accepted calendar precedence.
