# Auto-netting service design

## Scope

This candidate records proposed Day 2 service boundaries, APIs, and rule lifecycle mappings.

## Summary

The designs assign cashflow status changes to a lifecycle service and netting operations to a netting service.
They also map rule operations to event types, stored statuses, frontend actions, and rule actions.

### L3-AUTO-TECH-01 — Rule UI and service responsibilities

Reuse the netting-rule blotter with an auto-netting checkbox. Auto rules require booking entity, currency, and shifter; shifter supports hours and minutes. PendingAutoNetting cannot SettleAsGross. Static service calculates auto-netting time. Rule service supports isAutoNetting, update/delete, validation, duplication checks, and exclusions. A rule hit returns VD plus shifter and triggers IsAutoNettingEligible.

### L3-AUTO-TECH-02 — Lifecycle execution predicates

Lifecycle stores job_time and computes it from VD plus shifter. Query WAITING with PendingAutoNetting by booking entity, currency, counterparty, value date, and job time. Lock target cashflows. At or after job time, net groups larger than one. For exactly one, SettleAsSingle returns QUEUED and a Single Cashflow NSTP exception applies. Before job time, do nothing. Record execution results. The SingleCashflow XPath remains pending; naming differs from SettleAsSingle. Insert the orchestration step between netting eligibility and SSI stamping. The prose15-minute schedule and printed cron expression are unresolved.

### L3-AUTO-TECH-03 — Amendment-task ordering

The short process flow collects auto-netting tasks, removes amendment cashflows from those tasks, and then performs auto-netting. It specifies no matching identity, retained version, transaction boundary, retry, or race handling. Do not infer them from the ordering alone.
