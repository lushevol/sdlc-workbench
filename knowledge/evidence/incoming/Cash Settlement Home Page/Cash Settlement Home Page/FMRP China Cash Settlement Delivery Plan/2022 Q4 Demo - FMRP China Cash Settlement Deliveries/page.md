# Key functions delivered in Q4 2022:

1. Cashflow Materialization
2. Cashflow Netting/Un-net supported in Ratan
3. Cashflow lifecycle management through Ratan
4. Murex trade market events & cashflow handling

| Function | Description | Demo cashflow ids |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Cashflow Materialization | The materialization is built with below business cases: - Cashflow generated with **payment date <= T+5** is materialized immediately by Ratan - Cashflow generated with **payment date >T+5 **will remain the initial status 'PROJECTED' - There's regular job scheduled to scan the existing 'PROJECTED' cashflows and perform the materialization if the cashflow **payment date - 5BD** is today. | - FX Spot: 003390506523(CNH), 003390506524(USD) - FX Forward T+5: 003381506601(SGD), 003381506602(USD) - FX Forward T+6: 003381506621(JPY), 003381506622(USD) - 004381505414,004381505415: Imported early and payment date - 5BD is today (13th Jan 2023) |
| Cashflow Netting/Un-net | Ratan have built the capacity to net cashflows - Netting cashflow from different TP system (Stella/Murex 2.11) - Netting preview which shows the projection netting result before user perform the actual netting - Netting execution to generate the netting resultant cashflow - Un-Net | M00089776901,003378503003,003378503004 |
| Cashflow lifecycle management | Ratan had built the capacity to manage the cashflow lifecycle, below are some common cases of cashflow status moving. | 004381505411,004381505412,004381505413 - 004381505411: (PROJECTED→ QUEUED→ FAILED) - 004381505412: (PROJECTED→ QUEUED→ SUPPRESSED) - 004381505413: (PROJECTED→ QUEUED→ PENDING→ VALIDATED→ RELEASED→ SETTLED) |
| Murex trade market events & cashflow handling | The Murex 2.11 → Ratan cashflow interface is built with capacity to consume the cashflows generated from different Murex market events - New trade booking: New cashflow 01 generated and sent to Ratan - Trade C&R: Reversal cashflow 02(reversal of 01) and correction cashflow 03 populated, Ratan would hold the 03 as NSTP while waiting the reversal of 01 completed. | - New trade booking: 1. Murex new cashflow 91145251: Ratan Id M00091145251 - Trade C&R: 1. Murex reversal cashflow 91145252: Ratan map to withdrawal of M00091145251 2. Murex correction cashflow 91145253: Ratan hold as NSTP M00091145253. |
