**F2B MIS**

- Ability to generate MIS based on whether a Trade was STP or not F2B (i.e., client booked the trade and there was no touchpoint at SCB across FO, MO, TCG, Confirmation & Settlement)
- Ability to drill down and view the NSTP at a function level (FO / MO / TCG / Conf / Sett)

**Settlements MIS**

1. Ability to have a real time dashboard showing the status of cashflows

- Volume & Value with ability to drill down of Cashflows - Value Today, Tomorrow - Are close to cutoff (All / special currencies) - Missing SSI's for next 5 Business Days - Key Clients - High Value - Cashflows that missed payment cutoff - Failed Trades

2. Ability to generate MIS through a combination of

- STP / NSTP for Settlements at a Group Level and breakdown by Region / Country / Branch Level
- Breakdown by Client Type (Interbank / FI / Corporate etc) and Priority (P100)
- Breakdown by Settlement Method (CLS / INHOUSE / DVP / NET / PVP / UTIL / GROSS etc)
- Breakdown by Period (Monthly / Quarterly / Yearly)
- Breakdown by Product / Sub Product (FX→SPOT/FWD/SWAP/NDF, DEPO/LOAN, IRS, CCS, CDS etc)
- Breakdown by each Client
- Breakdown by Touchpoints (SSI Input, Netting, Splitting, exceptions handling)
- Breakdown by Cashflow status (Pending / Released / Settled etc)
