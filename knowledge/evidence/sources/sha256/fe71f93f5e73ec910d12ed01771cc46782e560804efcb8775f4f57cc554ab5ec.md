# **Pre-cab Engagement **

- Pre-cab meeting scheduled on each Wed for the coming weekend release.
- All the concern raised during early engagement must be closed.
- All working items should be approved by PO and TSO.
- Change 1st level approval would be given by SME by EOD Wed.
- Pre-CAB result will be sent to Jane for change 2nd level approval and work items approval by 10 AM Thu.
