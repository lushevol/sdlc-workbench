## Background

Migrate cashflow settlement from Murex Korea to RATAN by leveraging cashflows from existing Murex Korea trade population and future FMRP-migrated trades. This eliminates settlement processing dependency on Murex while establishing RATAN as the strategic platform for future settlement operations.

1. Migration of Murex-Korea payment processing to RATAN, the strategic cash settlements platform.
2. Enable SWIFT payment generation from RATAN to ENISIS, supporting Korea-specific payment validation prior to transmission to SAA.
3. Facilitate real-time settlement accounting posting from RATAN to OLTP.
4. Consolidation of Korea's client settlement instruction into SSI+ as the golden source.
5. Establish end-to-end automation of suspense account reconciliation through TLM.
