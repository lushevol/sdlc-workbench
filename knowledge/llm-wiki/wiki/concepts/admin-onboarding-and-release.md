# Administration, onboarding, and release

This topic connects application onboarding, administrative review, configuration promotion, technical release, and business activation. The linked records remain candidate or draft knowledge.

## Core model

- Onboarding joins frontend assets, backend routing, authentication, entitlement setup, and Portal catalog records. [[wb-candidate-operations-kt-admin-and-onboarding]]
- Maker-checker review and configuration promotion are separate from technical go-live and business activation. [[wb-candidate-operations-kt-admin-and-onboarding]] [[wb-candidate-operations-kt-release-operations]]
- Upload success cannot establish routing, connectivity, sign-off, UVT, or business readiness. [[wb-candidate-operations-kt-release-operations]]
- Interaction events describe adoption and UI context; workload measurements support separate performance claims with their own limits. [[wb-candidate-observability-kt-observability-and-performance]]

## Open boundaries

Release duration, urgent-release agreement, authentication dependencies, analytics retention, rollback behavior, and operating ownership need authoritative decisions and execution evidence. [[wb-candidate-operations-kt-release-operations]]
