# RATAN Entity and Currency Onboarding Controls

The generic checklist describes onboarding through entity, product, routing, accounting, Swift, static-data, and business-rule configuration. It assigns maker-checker controls to selected GUI changes and requires Ops-reviewed inputs or change requests for bulk updates. The Vietnam checklist remains an estimate with unapproved branch and product assumptions; its Data Entitlement owner is blank.

The retained checklists differ by date and cohort. Hefei, Tranche 1, Tranche 2, and Tranche 3 entries vary in legacy routing, H1/H2 calculation windows, SSI hierarchy, currency setup, and LMS feed expectations. A Hefei issue records branch-specific SSI flowing into Murex as global. “Done” labels and role names do not prove the work ran.

Currency onboarding covers legal-entity/currency cutoff, Nostro data, ISO mappings, precious-metal classification, and selected rounding configuration. Producer-specific Murex and Stella SSI notes retain different CFI stamping responsibilities. Confirm the applicable checklist, effective routing, and owners before treating any item as a current control.
