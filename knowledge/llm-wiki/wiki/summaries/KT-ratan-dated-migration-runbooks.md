# Dated China and UK migration runbooks

The China runbook targets 28 October 2024 payments. It describes entity, product, and date filters; Hold/WAITING actions; staged processing after migration; and temporary Murex withdrawal suppression. It also specifies dated workflow changes, reconciliation, signoff, optional scripts, and reversion. The product list retains a confirmation request. These instructions do not prove execution or current suppression policy.

The UK runbook describes a 19 January 2025 release, timeout configuration, Operations verification, CPT-control removal, scripted release or rejection, and later Murex publication. Some execution times remain unknown. The source does not prove completion, current script safety, or continuing authority.

The related [[summaries/KT-ratan-trade-migration-controls]] covers later environment-specific Murex/Stella migration controls. Do not substitute its PROD or DR rules for these dated runbooks.
