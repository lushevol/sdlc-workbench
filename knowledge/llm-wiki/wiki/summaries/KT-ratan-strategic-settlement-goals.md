# Strategic settlement goals and attribute dependencies

The project page calls for an in-house strategic settlement platform that removes vendor dependency, addresses pain points, and supports complex scenarios. It proposes reuse within TPT and describes desired ownership of post-trade actions, a payment lake for actions/data/audit, removal of NSP creation, reuse or extension of existing services, and one settlement source for release timing. These are source-stated goals, not demonstrated outcomes.

A separate dependency table links currency to precious-metal versus other SWIFT message types, rounding, payment SWIFT, and settlement accounting. It links entity to entity-specific rounding and Murex 2.11 NSTP rules. The table declares dependencies but does not provide complete mappings or establish implementation coverage.

The sources do not confirm current strategic scope, accountable ownership, or effective currency/entity mappings. Those mappings remain with their owning contracts; this summary does not supply missing values.
