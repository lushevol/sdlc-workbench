# Trade-date and exception-comment display

A January 2024 enhancement says a null trade date must not display today. It provides no alternative fallback and does not authorize rewriting the missing value.

A separate Q&A says comments entered during maker/checker exception handling appear in the cashflow audit table. It assigns already-netted Murex IRS handling to Murex and says China need not handle that case. This source does not establish a general cross-region prohibition.
