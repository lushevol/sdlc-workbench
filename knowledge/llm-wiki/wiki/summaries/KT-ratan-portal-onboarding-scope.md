# RATAN Portal onboarding request scope

> **Source status:** Candidate/draft. The checklists record requested onboarding scope, not signed approval, deployment, or business activation.

A July 2026 request proposes Markets Operations One branding, CDUPS confirmation blotters, Entra SSO by default, and an emergency fallback link. It also names login, logout, session extension, and Entra/EMS2 proxy interfaces. The current user-stated portal SSO rule prohibits OneMFA fallback. The historical emergency-link request does not supersede that rule. [PORTAL-SSO-AC-001](../../../records/candidates/security/KT-authentication-and-sessions.md#portal-sso-ac-001) [Branding, authentication, and confirmation tiles](../../../records/candidates/operations/KT-ratan-portal-onboarding-scope.md#branding-authentication-and-confirmation-tiles)

A separate desktop checklist requests OpenFin alongside browser access, without specifying replacement behavior, desktop authorization, or availability. An August 2026 checklist requests five FSS and two VPA tiles. The named tile list and separate request links define scope only; they do not prove technical onboarding, acceptance, or business enablement.
