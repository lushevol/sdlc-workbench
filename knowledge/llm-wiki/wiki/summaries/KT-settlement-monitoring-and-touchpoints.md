# Settlement monitoring and touchpoints

The MIS requirement measures end-to-end STP and function-level manual touchpoints. Proposed dashboards cover settlement volume/value near cutoffs, missing SSI, high value, failed trades, and priority clients. Missing-SSI monitoring looks five business days ahead. Breakdown dimensions include geography, client, settlement method, period, product, touchpoint, and status. Required attributes include cashflows, maker/checker actions, user profiles, client type, and P100 tagging. The source asks RATAN to feed a TPP report.

The touchpoint roadmap separates China Day 1 handling from target automation. Future items include external-venue netting, SSI selection, receipt-driven DVP release, and payment-gateway integration. Settlement-method authority, commodity handling, mandatory currency identifiers, and some manual workflows remain unresolved.

Existing-state descriptions do not establish target implementation. The source does not identify approved dashboard measures, cutoff definitions, drilldowns, or rollout targets. No monitoring or live feed was verified.
