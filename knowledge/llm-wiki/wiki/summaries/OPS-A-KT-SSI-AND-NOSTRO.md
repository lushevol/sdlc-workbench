# SSI and Nostro Operations

The record groups dated requests for Swift-message display, Vostro lookup, Nostro effective dates, SSI selection, CFI stamping, and onboarding. The regional checklists differ: HK/TW and Prime expect one or two SSI agents and exclude three; Korea requests SGO-to-SGD and KRO-to-KRW conversion while its Nostro row strikes out SGO conversion. Nostro sources and values remain TBC in several checklists.

The DOI SSI-ID rules distinguish automatic stamping, manual Vostro entry, selection, and later edits. Selecting an unchanged SI assigns its SSI ID. For automatic or selected SI with an SSI ID, changes limited to fields 70/72 retain that ID, but other edits remove it; manually restoring the displayed values does not restore the ID. Maker selection and checker re-entry are separate paths and can fail validation despite matching displayed values.

The 2026 retrospective requests hard stops for missing mandatory NSTP attributes. Those requests, test plans, and “expected” checklist rows do not prove delivery. Confirm effective SSI hierarchy, validation scope, and regional static mappings before treating these statements as current controls.
