# AG Grid

AG Grid is a data-grid product. In the source material it appears as one of three candidates in a historical user-interface library comparison, together with [[entities/mui]] and [[entities/ant-design]].

The comparison did not focus on AG Grid alone. It assessed the candidates across components, styling, licensing, and related criteria, and recorded GitHub statistics dated 5 July 2023.

## Key facts recorded in the source

- AG Grid is grouped with [[entities/mui]] and [[entities/ant-design]] in a single evaluation table.
- The evaluation criteria include component coverage, styling approach, and licensing terms.
- The recorded statistics are dated 5 July 2023. They describe the product at that time, not its present state.
- A related conceptual-design draft keeps unresolved rationale and selection placeholders. It does not record a completed selection or licensing decision for AG Grid.
- The material does not say whether AG Grid is used in [[entities/fmo-post-trade-portal]].

## Status and evidence limits

This is an evaluation record, not an accepted architecture decision. It cannot establish current AG Grid features, current licence terms, or an approved purchasing outcome. Any statement about adoption, version, or grid usage in the portal needs a decision record and a deployed implementation as evidence.
