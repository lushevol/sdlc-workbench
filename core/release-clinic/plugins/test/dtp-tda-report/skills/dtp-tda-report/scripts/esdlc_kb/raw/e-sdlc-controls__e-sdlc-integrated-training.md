# Controls: E-SDLC Integrated Training

---

## Control Attributes

| **Control Name** | Integrated Training |
| --- | --- |
| **L3 Risk Taxonomy** | Process Governance |
| **Control Objective** | - All parties involved in E-SDLC are trained. - To create awareness of the E-SDLC framework and to improve compliance |
| **Control Requirements** |<br/>- Ensure Integrated training is in place to cover end to end E-SDLC process<br/>- Ensure SDLC Integrated trainings are enforced to key Personas involved in the technology delivery<br/>- Ensure QPR/Hive level reports on overall compliance ratings are published and relevant actions are taken to uplift the compliance posture |
| **Control Tasks** |<br/>- Each persona based training will emphasize both E-SDLC process adherence and ADO usage:<br/>- Product Owner Persona (Product Owner)<br/>- Testing Persona (Test Engineer, Test Manager)<br/>- Security Persona (Security Champion, Security Control owner, Security Assurance, CISO)<br/>- Development Persona (Engineer/Developer, Development Lead)<br/>- Architect Persona (Solution Architect, Domain Architect )<br/>- Review training compliance & engineering maturity of each QPR/Hive<br/>- Uplift training threshold for underperforming/less matured QPR/Hives based on the number of non-compliance/exceptions |
| **Evidence** | The controls expectations must be met using the Bank's standard process & tools. Training status and certification details are to be centrally maintained by the E-SDLC Governance team and be available for all Internal and External assessments, CIO teams and Risk reporting. |
